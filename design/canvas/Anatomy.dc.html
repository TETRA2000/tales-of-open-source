Three pointers make a tree

A fiber points only to its first child. Brothers and sisters hang off sibling, and everyone can climb back up through return. That shape lets the work loop walk the tree without recursion — and pause anywhere.

Two trees, one screen

React keeps the tree on screen (current) and builds the next one beside it (workInProgress). Each fiber’s alternate points at its twin. On commit, the root simply swaps which tree is current.
